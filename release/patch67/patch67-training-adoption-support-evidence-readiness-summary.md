# Patch 67 Training Adoption and Support Evidence Readiness

Patch 67 adds read-only training, adoption, and support evidence readiness guidance to Production Evidence Closure.

## Scope

- Adds training/adoption/support evidence readiness helper logic.
- Shows missing adoption evidence summary, owner/reviewer readiness, due-date or overdue state, source workflow destination, and executive impact.
- Adds training/adoption/support signals to the evidence intake queue, evidence detail panel, department register, and executive closure pack.
- Updates Production Operator Console wording to point operators to training, adoption, and support evidence readiness.
- Extends release noise restore coverage to the Patch 66 generated proof JSON.

## Safety

- No migration was added.
- No backend write endpoint was added.
- No direct closure action was added.
- No evidence is auto-closed or marked verified.
- Missing or unclear training, adoption, or support evidence remains visible as evidence required or review required.
